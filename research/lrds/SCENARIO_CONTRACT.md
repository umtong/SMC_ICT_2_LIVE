# LRDS causal scenario contract

## Purpose

LRDS models one liquidity event as a hierarchy of three different owners:

1. **Public root hypothesis** — why the market can continue or rotate from the accepted-auction context toward a natural liquidity destination.
2. **Control attempt** — whether one concrete Source can authorize capital at the current location.
3. **Owned position premise** — the immutable Source, information exit, root invalidation and destination currently owned by the one global account slot.

A local candle pattern cannot silently own all three levels.

## Continuation and rotation

A liquidity contact starts competing continuation and rotation explanations. Root ownership must be supplied by independent market-state evidence, such as pre-retest initiative, already-live price discovery, a break of structure that existed before the contact, accepted-value migration, auction reclaim, or a direct control shift.

A tactical retest or local CSD may prove a Control Source, but it does not by itself prove the root market state.

## Control Source lifecycle

A fresh Source follows one causal sequence:

1. the Source exists after a completed MSS/CSD/causal impulse;
2. a **later completed body** trades wholly away from the Source;
3. a **later first return** reaches it;
4. the return does not itself authorize capital;
5. a **later completed thesis-side body** reclaims the whole pullback body and renews delivery;
6. the resulting proof may authorize capital once.

The creation bar cannot be the first retest. The separation bar cannot be the first retest. A consumed or failed proof cannot be reused.

## Entry and sizing

Entry requires an active root hypothesis and a defended Control Source. The natural objective must remain ahead and the conservative route must retain at least 0.5R after expected round-trip fees and slippage.

Normal planned loss is fixed at:

```text
NAV × 3%
```

Quantity is that budget divided by the expected per-unit loss from actual decision price to the **root premise invalidation**, including expected costs. There is no nominal cap, arbitrary leverage cap, or model-score risk multiplier.

## Holding and exit

- A Control Source failure exits the current full position and retires that proof. The public root hypothesis may remain observable.
- Root auction/route invalidation exits the position and invalidates the root branch.
- Natural objective completion exits the full position unless a separately confirmed child Source owns a farther destination.
- A child Source can transfer information-exit ownership and tighten—but never widen—the emergency boundary. Quantity is unchanged.
- There are no partial entries, partial exits, or forced time exits.

## ML boundary

ML may estimate latent state, evidence reliability and action value inside this explicit causal contract. It may not invent a second signal family, backdate market objects, reuse a consumed proof, or override an invalid premise.
