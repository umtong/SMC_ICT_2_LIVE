# Ethereum principal-scale validator exit-flow Core

This study continues from the economically failed aggregate validator-withdrawal policy without retuning its price response. It separates the verified EIP-4895 source into two protocol-scale streams before opening new market outcomes:

- **principal-scale:** withdrawal amount at least 16 ETH;
- **partial-reward control:** withdrawal amount below 16 ETH.

The 16 ETH boundary is a conservative source-semantic proxy fixed before the split result. It is not claimed to identify every exact validator exit. The source job verifies chronology, global withdrawal-index continuity and row/ETH conservation. Only a passing split may open the frozen Bybit ETHUSDT action-value screen.

The economic screen uses identical source timing, market state, structural long/short actions, funding, cost, risk and global-slot logic for both streams. Development and confirmation are stage-bounded: unresolved positions are marked at the boundary and never supply labels across the next stage. The principal stream must be positive, broad, winner-resistant and superior to the partial-reward control in both forward stages to proceed.

The result is explicitly low-confidence and exposed because aggregate withdrawal outcomes for the same 2023 forward months were already observed. No credentials or orders are used.
