This material was used only to formulate a falsifiable hypothesis. It is not treated as proof of an executable edge.

Research translation:

- compare four pre-known anchor prices under identical target, stop, execution and cost rules;
- require the New-York midnight anchor to be absolutely cost-positive and uniquely superior before any session, FVG/BPR, OI or ML extension.
