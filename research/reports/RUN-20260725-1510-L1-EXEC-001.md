## Main finding

The development-selected gate used a 50% queue-ahead assumption, a three-second TTL, prior five-second side-flow of at least -0.25, non-adverse side microprice, and quote-update intensity no greater than four times its prior median.

It reduced mean five-second round-trip execution drag by 0.200 bp in development, 0.199 bp in validation, and 0.149 bp in confirmation. The routed result nevertheless remained about -11.8 bp without an external directional alpha.

## Taker Slippage-at-Risk

At 250 ms modeled submission latency, the mean acquisition cost including the configured six-basis-point taker fee was about 6.02 bp. The average 99th-percentile cost was approximately 6.94–7.16 bp across chronological splits. These values are research proxies because the source contains exchange event time but not local receive time.

## Multi-day state check

The nine separate ten-second days reproduced a small positive gross post-entry markout for the development-selected state, but after the configured maker-entry and taker-exit costs the thirty-second proxy remained about -7.53 to -7.57 bp in all three month blocks.

## Evidence boundaries

- Raw bookTicker covers one partial 2023-05-16 session per symbol.
- Nine separate ten-second days validate state-toxicity signs but not exact queue fills.
- Exchange timestamps only; no local receive time or private execution.
- Queue-ahead and order size are modeled from displayed L1 quantity.
- This experiment measures execution drag and is not a standalone directional-alpha test.

## Decision

- Reject as standalone alpha.
- Retain only as an execution gate for prospective Shadow.
- Do not change Champion or live-order permission.
- Require forward local-receive timestamps, actual account fees, observed partial fills, queue reconciliation, and private execution events before promotion.
