# Method sources

The audit design uses primary or official sources only.

- Guillaume, Viallon & Lerasle, *SurvivalBoost: Using Boosting to Predict
  Competing Risks*, AISTATS 2025 (PMLR 258). Role: proper scoring and
  probability prediction under censoring and competing events.
- scikit-learn official documentation, *Probability calibration*. Role:
  calibration data must be independent of the data used to fit the base model.
- Grødem et al., *Calibration in competing risks*, 2026 preprint. Role:
  cause-specific calibration must be evaluated explicitly rather than inferred
  from discrimination alone.

These sources determine the audit method, not a claim that the project strategy
is profitable.
