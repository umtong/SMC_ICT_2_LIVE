## Question

- total OI increases between the first and second defended pivot;
- sell-account share rises for equal highs or buy-account share rises for equal lows;

## Interpretation

The causal addition was informative but insufficient. Growing OI plus a rising defending account share identifies a better subset of raids than geometry alone. It still does not reveal position size by side, entry-price concentration, hedge relationships or which side of the OI change is actually closing. The failure is therefore not “OI has no information”; it is that aggregate OI/account ratios do not resolve forced inventory precisely enough for a winner-independent day-trading policy.

The raid-release rejection map was especially informative: the intuitive rule “defenders were forced out, so the failed raid should reverse” lost broadly. A contraction in aggregate OI or account share is not proof that the relevant vulnerable cohort has been exhausted.

## Decision

`RETIRE_INVENTORY_SPONSORED_EQUAL_POOL_PRE2024`.

Do not rescue this family with OI magnitude thresholds, account-ratio thresholds, another accumulation window, pool tolerance, action geometry, target, stop, session, cost, risk, leverage or ML. The next information source must observe forced flow or side-specific executable replenishment directly; price plus aggregate inventory is insufficient. Ranking and live authority remain unchanged.
