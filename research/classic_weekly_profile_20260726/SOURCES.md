# Sources and quantitative translation

The sources define SMC/ICT vocabulary only; they are not evidence of profitability.

- **The Inner Circle Trader — Trading Plan Development 6** (`s9bg8JF7rm8`, 2017-12-24): short-term trading-plan and market-maker delivery context.
- **The Inner Circle Trader — Market Maker Series Vol. 3 of 5** (`i8xt0EQDjNY`, 2021-07-30): market structure, key levels and delivery cycles.
- **Bybit public MT4 kline archive**: official historical completed OHLCV input.

## Frozen translation

| SMC/ICT vocabulary | Causal observable |
|---|---|
| weekly accumulation | complete Monday or Monday–Tuesday UTC range |
| Judas manipulation | next-day range-extreme raid by a fixed hourly-ATR distance |
| CISD | close through the open of the last opposite-colour hourly candle known before the raid |
| displacement | hourly candle body and close-location thresholds |
| classic buy/sell week | low/high raid followed by opposite-direction weekly delivery |
| draw on liquidity | opposite early-week boundary or prior-week external high/low |

The code does not infer hidden intent. Every range, candle, sweep, entry, stop and target is timestamp-defined before the outcome.
