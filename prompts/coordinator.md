# Coordinator prompt

Normalize data, cost, execution, and evaluation conditions before comparison. Reject stale, duplicate, conflicting, or invalid results explicitly. Merge only supported state changes, increment the revision, preserve the previous state as a snapshot when material, and assign non-overlapping next tasks. Do not re-explain settled goals.
